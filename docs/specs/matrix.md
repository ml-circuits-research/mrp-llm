# Specification Matrix

The specifications DS000 to DS028 are numbered without gaps and listed here in file order. DS024 to DS028 define the MRP-LLM layer (SOP surfaces, Models and strategies, the language pipeline, the model gateway settings, schemas and discovery); DS000 to DS023 come from the ChatSOP symbolic core. They were renumbered on 2026-10-01 when the tiny-model research branch was frozen; its specifications (training, data and evaluation, query curriculum, independent and research corpora, corpus audit, capability APIs) are archived in `probably_obsolete/tinyLLMExperiments/specs/`; the symbolic EmotionDetectionSystem was restored as DS023 on 2026-10-02. `aliases.json` maps every former id to its current spec, the spec it was merged into, or its archive location. Former ids remain only in frozen files (archives, the project journal, the append-only topic notes and historical reports), which keep the numbering of their date; `node tools/check-spec-refs.mjs` enforces this.
