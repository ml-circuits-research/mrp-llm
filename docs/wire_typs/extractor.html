extractor

An extraction recipe of a schema (DS028): the formalize strategy that extracts its instances, the triggers that select it, the Ploinky task of its prompt and the wire types it is expected to write.

Status and author: Knowledge wire of a base memory, written by a person or proposed by a discover strategy. The routed formalizers SpladeRouted and SaeRouted select extractors by their triggers (external strategies); SchemaGuidedLLM reads the schema itself.

Accepted fields

Single means at most once, repeatable means zero or more, and required means at least once. Other field names are rejected (unknown_field). The table is generated from the grammar in sop/knowledge/grammar.mjs.

FieldCardinalityAccepted values and behavior
schemaExactly once; required~id of a schema wire (schema_member_type when the wire is not a schema).
strategyExactly once; requiredOne of SchemaGuidedLLM SpladeRouted SaeRouted DependencyScaffold.
triggerRepeatable; optionalText: a term, an SAE feature or a pattern that selects this extractor.
taskAt most once; optionalText: the name of the Ploinky task (config/tasks/NAME.md) of its prompt.
outputRepeatable; optionalA lowercase symbol: a wire type the extraction is expected to write (query, stated, ...).
sourceAt most once; optionalText: where the recipe comes from.

Examples

@works_at predicate
  args subject:entity object:entity
  label en "works at"

@s_company schema
  name "company"
  predicate ~works_at

@ex_company extractor
  schema ~s_company
  strategy SchemaGuidedLLM
  trigger "work"
  task "mrp-formalize-one-shot"
  output query

Invalid: a strategy that does not extract

@works_at predicate
  args subject:entity object:entity
  label en "works at"

@s_company schema
  name "company"
  predicate ~works_at

@ex_bad extractor
  schema ~s_company
  strategy Guess
