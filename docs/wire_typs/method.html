method

The manual's own procedure for a task: an ordered list of steps with the choice points the engine may optimise, in the style of hierarchical task networks. "To back up a server, lock the database, take a snapshot (or a dump if the database is small), then unlock it."

Status and author: Knowledge wire, written by a model or the owner from a source and stored by the host after review. A model-origin program cannot author it (the model-origin compiler rejects it). Governed: its `version`, `supersedes` and `approval` fields decide which version binds. Methods are the "mode of work" half of a procedure; the norms that bound them are norm wires.

Accepted fields

Single means at most once, repeatable means zero or more, and required means at least once. Other field names are rejected (unknown_field). The table is generated from the grammar in sop/knowledge/grammar.mjs.

FieldCardinalityAccepted values and behavior
achievesAt most once; requiredThe task atom this method achieves (backup_done ?s). Its variables are the method's parameters.
whenRepeatable; optionalA guard: a condition group that must hold for the method to apply.
stepRepeatable; requiredOne step per line, in order; a block opens on the line and closes with end. Forms: ~action term... (arity must match the action's params: step_arity_mismatch), a sub-task atom (some approved method must achieve it: subtask_without_method), achieve ATOM, optional STEP, choose (two or more alternatives, one per line: choose_needs_alternatives), any_order, if ATOM ... else ... end, until ATOM max N ... end (max is mandatory: until_needs_max), pick ?x where ATOM.
preferRepeatable; optional~a over ~b: between equal-cost alternatives, prefer a.
on_failureAt most once; optional$method, replan or abort: what to do when the method cannot proceed. Under binding strict this is the only way out of the method.
triggered_byAt most once; optionalAn event atom that triggers the method.
bindingAt most once; optionalstrict (default: the engine may not plan from primitives when an approved method exists, and may leave the method only through on_failure) or advisory (the method guides the search and the engine may fall back). Write advisory only when the source says "recommended".
costAt most once; optionalA non-negative integer cost of using the method.
sourceAt most once; optionalQuoted citation of the manual section.
versionAt most once; optionalA positive integer. A revision is a new wire with version N+1 and supersedes $old; the old wire is never edited.
supersedesAt most once; optionalA $id of a wire of the same type with a lower version (supersedes_type_mismatch, version_not_increasing). An approved successor makes its predecessor approval superseded.
approvalAt most once; optionalproposed, approved, contested, rejected, superseded or retired. Written by the host when it accepts the wire; an author never writes it (authoring mode ignores it with governance_ignored). Only approved wires bind, and so do contested ones, flagged.
approved_byAt most once; optionalText. Written by the host at approval; required with approval approved in memory (approval_incomplete).
approved_atAt most once; optionalISO date or timestamp. Written by the host; asof selects the version in force from this date. Required for every norm and every strict method.
retired_atAt most once; optionalISO date or timestamp after which the wire no longer binds.
scopeAt most once; optionalA quoted list of tags (scope "ops, business_hours"). A scoped wire binds when its tags intersect the query context; an unscoped wire always binds.
quoteAt most once; optionalThe exact words of the source, copied byte for byte.

What it is for and how to write it

Write each step the manual gives, in order, with the primitive action wires it names (each with its preconditions and effects). Put the manual's own branching in if, choose and optional; the engine chooses only at the choice points you write. Branching beyond that is several methods with guards. Every loop has max; an unbounded loop cannot be expressed and goes into the report as "not expressed". "Always do it this way" is binding strict; "recommended" is binding advisory; an omitted binding is strict.

StepMeaning
~action ?xa primitive step
task ?xa sub-task, achieved by another method
achieve ATOMthe planner fills the gap between the state and the atom
optional STEPthe engine may skip it, and does when it costs more than it helps
choose ... endone alternative per line, at least two
any_order ... endthe enclosed steps in any order
if ATOM ... else ... endbranch on the state reached before the step
until ATOM max N ... endbounded iteration
pick ?x where ATOMthe engine chooses a binding

What the engine does

A plan is a run of the program: the legal executions are all the ways of resolving the choice points (choose, pick, any_order, optional, achieve, and the if outcomes fixed by the state). mode plan returns the cheapest legal execution that satisfies the norms in force, ties broken by prefer, with the choices it made in choices. A depth or iteration cap that is hit gives budget_exhausted, not a plan. mode procedure renders the approved method of a task as of asof without planning. Methods are planned by the strategies that declare method; the oracle js-reference declares them not_expressible.

Examples

@server predicate
  args subject:entity
@big_db predicate
  args subject:entity
@locked predicate
  args subject:entity
@image predicate
  args subject:entity
@backup_done predicate
  args subject:entity

@lock_db action
  params ?s
  requires server ?s
  adds locked ?s
  cost 1
@snapshot action
  params ?s
  requires locked ?s
  adds image ?s
  cost 2
@dump action
  params ?s
  requires locked ?s
  adds image ?s
  cost 4
@unlock_db action
  params ?s
  requires image ?s
  requires locked ?s
  adds backup_done ?s
  removes locked ?s
  cost 1

@s1 fact
  holds server s1
@do_backup method
  achieves backup_done ?s
  when server ?s
  binding strict
  step ~lock_db ?s
  step if big_db ?s
    ~snapshot ?s
  else
    ~dump ?s
  end
  step ~unlock_db ?s
@q query
  mode plan
  where backup_done s1

The question is validated and, by a strategy that declares method, planned; the oracle answers not_expressible.

Invalid: a loop without a bound

@ready predicate
  args subject:entity
@wait action
  params ?s
  requires ready ?s
  adds ready ?s
@m method
  achieves ready ?s
  when ready ?s
  step until ready ?s
    ~wait ?s
  end

Common mistakes

  1. A ~step with the wrong number of terms for its action (step_arity_mismatch).
  2. choose with one alternative, or until without max.
  3. A sub-task atom that no method achieves (subtask_without_method).
  4. Writing advisory for a step the manual makes mandatory, or leaving a mandatory step to a strict method that a strict hard norm forbids (strict_forbid_conflicts_method).
  5. Writing approval, approved_by or approved_at: the host writes them.
