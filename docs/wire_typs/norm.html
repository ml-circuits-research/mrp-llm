norm

What may and must be done: a prohibition, an obligation or a permission over an action or a state, with one temporal qualifier. "Do not hard-reset a router during business hours." "Notify the on-call engineer within 10 minutes." "In an emergency a hard reset is allowed."

Status and author: Knowledge wire, written by a model or the owner from a source and stored by the host after review. A model-origin program cannot author it (the model-origin compiler rejects it). Governed: its `version`, `supersedes` and `approval` fields decide which version binds. A norm is to actions and trajectories what integrity is to states.

Accepted fields

Single means at most once, repeatable means zero or more, and required means at least once. Other field names are rejected (unknown_field). The table is generated from the grammar in sop/knowledge/grammar.mjs.

FieldCardinalityAccepted values and behavior
forbidAt most once; optionalThe forbidden pattern: ~action term... or a state atom. Exactly one of forbid, oblige, permit (norm_one_modality).
obligeAt most once; optionalThe required pattern. A variable of the pattern must be bound by a when atom (unsafe_variable).
permitAt most once; optionalThe permitted pattern; it must name the prohibition it excepts in overrides (warning permit_without_target).
whenRepeatable; optionalThe condition under which the norm applies; the pattern's variables count as bound.
withinAt most once; optionalA positive integer: an obligation met within N steps (time units in a timestamped trace) of its trigger. At most one temporal qualifier (norm_qualifier_conflict).
beforeAt most once; optional~b: a prohibition before b has occurred, or an obligation to occur before b occurs.
afterAt most once; optional~b: a prohibition once b has occurred, or a required follow-up after b.
alwaysAt most once; optionalA flag. Prohibition (default): the pattern holds at no step. Obligation: the state atom is maintained at every step from the trigger.
sometimeAt most once; optionalA flag, obligations only: the pattern occurs at some step after the trigger.
at_most_onceAt most once; optionalA flag, prohibitions only: at most one occurrence in the run.
standingAt most once; optionalA flag, obligations only: the duty holds for every binding of the when, whatever the goal (standing_needs_oblige elsewhere). The validator warns obligation_unscoped once so that the author confirms the intent.
severityAt most once; optionalhard (default: a violating plan is invalid) or soft (a violation costs cost and is reported).
costAt most once; optionalA positive integer price of violating a soft norm (cost_needs_soft).
priorityAt most once; optionalAn integer; prefer overrides.
overridesRepeatable; optionalA $norm this norm excepts: the same subject and opposite force (overrides_target_mismatch), no cycles (overrides_cycle).
bindingAt most once; optionalstrict (default: the engine never relaxes it; the answer is blocked with blocked_by) or advisory (may be relaxed when the goal is otherwise blocked, and then listed in relaxed). Vacuous on a soft norm (binding_on_soft_norm).
messageAt most once; optionalThe reason string returned when the norm blocks a plan.
sourceAt most once; optionalQuoted citation of the sentence the norm comes from.
versionAt most once; optionalA positive integer. A revision is a new wire with version N+1 and supersedes $old; the old wire is never edited.
supersedesAt most once; optionalA $id of a wire of the same type with a lower version (supersedes_type_mismatch, version_not_increasing). An approved successor makes its predecessor approval superseded.
approvalAt most once; optionalproposed, approved, contested, rejected, superseded or retired. Written by the host when it accepts the wire; an author never writes it (authoring mode ignores it with governance_ignored). Only approved wires bind, and so do contested ones, flagged.
approved_byAt most once; optionalText. Written by the host at approval; required with approval approved in memory (approval_incomplete).
approved_atAt most once; optionalISO date or timestamp. Written by the host; asof selects the version in force from this date. Required for every norm and every strict method.
retired_atAt most once; optionalISO date or timestamp after which the wire no longer binds.
scopeAt most once; optionalA quoted list of tags (scope "ops, business_hours"). A scoped wire binds when its tags intersect the query context; an unscoped wire always binds.
quoteAt most once; optionalThe exact words of the source, copied byte for byte.

What it is for and how to write it

Pick the modality from the source's verb: "must not" is forbid, "must" is oblige, "may" is permit. State the pattern as ~action term... (an action must exist) or as a state atom, add the when that scopes it, and at most one qualifier. Say binding advisory only when the source says "recommended" or "should"; an omitted binding is strict, which is the fail-safe choice. A permission must name the prohibition it excepts.

ModalityQualifierViolated when
forbidalways (default)the pattern holds at some step
forbidbefore ~b / after ~bthe pattern occurs before any b / after a b
forbidat_most_oncethe pattern occurs a second time
obligesometimethe run ends with the trigger raised and no occurrence
obligealwaysthe state atom fails at a step from the trigger
obligewithin NN steps pass after the trigger with no occurrence
obligebefore ~b / after ~bb occurs first / the run ends after b with no later occurrence
permitnonenever; it blocks the forbid it overrides where its when holds

What the engine does

Obligation instances. An obligation is triggered per instance: the first step at which its when holds for a binding that comes from the goal's arguments or from an executed action. A binding that exists only because a row of memory satisfies the when creates no instance, so "notify router r7" binds to the router the question or a step concerns, not to every router in memory; standing says otherwise and is reported. Severity and binding are independent. hard makes a violating plan invalid, soft adds its cost; strict is never relaxed, advisory may be and is then listed in relaxed. A hard strict violation with no alternative gives blocked with blocked_by [norm id]. Conflicts. Two norms of equal strength in conflict give blocked with both ids under strict, never a silent pick. A permit blocks the prohibition it names for the arguments where its when holds. Audit. mode conform judges a performed trace against the versions in force at the time of each step.

Examples

@router predicate
  args subject:entity
@business_hours predicate
  args none
@emergency predicate
  args subject:entity
@notified predicate
  args subject:entity
@reset_done predicate
  args subject:entity

@notify_oncall action
  params ?r
  requires router ?r
  adds notified ?r
  cost 1
@hard_reset action
  params ?r
  requires router ?r
  adds reset_done ?r
  cost 1

@no_hard_reset_in_hours norm
  forbid ~hard_reset ?r
  when business_hours
  severity hard
  binding strict
  source "Ops manual 4.3"
@notify_promptly norm
  oblige ~notify_oncall ?r
  when router ?r
  within 10
  severity soft
  cost 50
@emergency_hard_reset norm
  permit ~hard_reset ?r
  when emergency ?r
  overrides $no_hard_reset_in_hours

The prohibition is hard and strict, the obligation is soft with a cost, and the permission excepts the prohibition in an emergency. Norms are evaluated by the strategies that declare norms_hard and norms_soft; the oracle declares them not_expressible.

Invalid: a norm with two modalities

@a predicate
  args subject:entity
@act action
  params ?x
  requires a ?x
  adds a ?x
@n norm
  forbid ~act ?x
  oblige ~act ?x

Warning: a permission that excepts nothing

@a predicate
  args subject:entity
@act action
  params ?x
  requires a ?x
  adds a ?x
@p norm
  permit ~act ?x

Common mistakes

  1. Two modalities, or two temporal qualifiers, in one norm.
  2. An oblige variable that no when atom binds, or a standing marker written only to silence the warning.
  3. A permit without overrides, or overriding a norm of the same force.
  4. cost on a hard norm (cost_needs_soft) or binding on a soft one (vacuous).
  5. Writing binding advisory for "must": an advisory norm can be relaxed.
  6. A strict hard forbid of an action that a strict method requires (strict_forbid_conflicts_method).
