reflex

A prepared reaction of a Symbolic Model (DS025 "Reflex boundary"), tried by the reflex slot before formalization: a trigger and a reply mode. stop answers and ends the turn, continue puts the reply before the computed answer, pragmatic only gives the voice a tone line.

Status and author: Knowledge wire of a base memory, written by a person and admitted through the knowledge validator or the review queue, like any other knowledge. The reflex wires of a Model's base memory count together with the pattern reflexes of the Model's own settings.

Accepted fields

Single means at most once, repeatable means zero or more, and required means at least once. Other field names are rejected (unknown_field). The table is generated from the grammar in sop/knowledge/grammar.mjs.

FieldCardinalityAccepted values and behavior
patternAt most once; optionalA JSON-quoted, case-insensitive regular expression over the English message (bad_pattern when it does not compile).
affectAt most once; optionalOne of frustrated urgent confused grateful angry happy sad: the reflex fires when the affect slot reads that label with intensity at least 0.5. A reflex needs a pattern, an affect label or both (reflex_without_trigger); with both, both must hold.
modeAt most once; optionalOne of stop continue pragmatic (default stop).
replyAt most once; optionalText: the reply of stop and continue (reflex_without_reply when missing).
toneAt most once; optionalText: the tone line of pragmatic (reflex_without_reply when missing).
priorityAt most once; optionalAn integer; a higher priority is tried first (default 0).
sourceAt most once; optionalText: where the reflex comes from.

Examples

@rx_hours reflex
  pattern "opening hours|when are you open"
  mode stop
  reply "The office is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00."
  priority 2

@rx_urgent reflex
  affect urgent
  mode continue
  reply "I see this is urgent; here is what I found."

Invalid: no trigger

@rx_bad reflex
  mode stop
  reply "Hello."
