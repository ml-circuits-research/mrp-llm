schema

A value schema of a Symbolic Model (DS028): the predicates of one domain, the lexemes that trigger them and the kinds of entity they are about, with who proposed it, a version and a review status. A reviewed schema is the closed vocabulary of the formalize strategy SchemaGuidedLLM: its prompt offers only these predicates and the validator refuses any other.

Status and author: Knowledge wire of a base memory, written by a person (provenance manual) or proposed by a discover strategy (POST /v1/mrp/models/{id}/discover) and approved through the review queue. The small model never authors it.

Accepted fields

Single means at most once, repeatable means zero or more, and required means at least once. Other field names are rejected (unknown_field). The table is generated from the grammar in sop/knowledge/grammar.mjs.

FieldCardinalityAccepted values and behavior
nameExactly once; requiredA JSON-quoted display name of the schema.
domainAt most once; optionalA lowercase symbol naming the domain.
predicateRepeatable; required~id of a predicate wire of the memory or its imports (unknown_ref when there is none, schema_member_type when the wire is not a predicate).
lexemeRepeatable; optional~id of a lexeme wire that triggers one of the predicates (schema_member_type when the wire is not a lexeme).
entity_typeRepeatable; optionalA lowercase symbol: a kind of entity the schema is about.
provenanceAt most once; optionalOne of manual splade-stats llm-consolidate frame-seed sae-llm-activations sae-sentence-embeddings sae-pretrained-dictionary (default manual).
versionAt most once; optionalA positive integer; a re-run of discovery proposes a new version, never overwrites.
statusAt most once; optionalOne of proposed reviewed deprecated (default reviewed). Only reviewed schemas guide formalization.
descriptionAt most once; optionalText: what the schema covers.
sourceAt most once; optionalText: where the schema comes from.

What it is for and how to write it

List the predicates the Model's questions are about. A predicate stays the contract with the reasoners and with rules written by people: once reviewed it is never renamed; deprecate the schema and add a new version instead.

Examples

@works_at predicate
  args subject:entity object:entity
  label en "works at"

@lx_works_at_en lexeme
  of works_at
  language en
  form "work at"
  frame subject object

@s_company schema
  name "company"
  domain company
  provenance manual
  version 1
  status reviewed
  predicate ~works_at
  lexeme ~lx_works_at_en
  entity_type person

Invalid: a member that is not a predicate

@ana entity
  label en "Ana"

@s_bad schema
  name "bad"
  predicate ~ana
