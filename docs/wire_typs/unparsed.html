Wire index · stated · assumed · query · unclear · clarify · model/host roles

unparsed

An unparsed wire is the small model's honest report of a part of the message it could not formalize: a verbatim span, the wire it belongs to and the slot it probably fills. It belongs to the model language (the sixth model wire type). The model formalizes everything else as usual and marks only the span; the host then tries deterministic repairs, and whatever stays unresolved becomes one targeted question per span, while the rest of the message is still answered. The model never drops, paraphrases or guesses a part it did not understand. The normative contract is DS014 ("Honest partial formalization").

unparsed is not unclear: unclear kind gibberish or no_request describes the whole message and stays the only wire of the output, while any number of unparsed wires accompany the wires the model could write. It is also not clarify: the model never asks; the host generates the question.

Every accepted keyword

KeywordCardinality / defaultAccepted value and concrete effect
spanExactly once; requiredOne nonempty JSON-quoted verbatim part of the message, at most 200 characters (unparsed_span_form). The agent guard checks that it occurs in the message after case and whitespace folding (unparsed_span_not_in_message); it is never a paraphrase or a translation. Two unparsed wires never carry the same span (unparsed_duplicate).
nearAt most once; optionalnear $id: the stated, assumed, query or constraint wire the span belongs to (unparsed_near_type for an unparsed or unclear wire or the wire itself; an unknown name fails the graph check). Without near the span belongs to the whole message: the host tries to repair it and otherwise asks, holding nothing back.
hintAt most once; optionalThe slot the span probably fills, from a closed set: a role name subject, object, time or location; value (a number, an amount, any other value); relation (the near wire's relation phrase is the model's best attempt and the host also tries the span); reference (something said earlier in the conversation); other. Any other word fails parsing. The hint pairs the span with a placeholder and chooses the repair and the wording of the question.

Placeholders

When the value of a role lies in an unparsed span, that role holds a fresh placeholder ?variable, also in stated and assumed, and the unparsed wire carries near $<that wire> and hint <role name>. A generic hint (value, reference, other) or no hint pairs with the placeholder when it is the only such span near the wire. A placeholder in a statement without exactly one matching span is rejected (proposition_not_ground): a statement states what the message says. In a query, a span near the query with a role hint pairs with the one non-selected variable of that role; the question still asks what it asks.

Host repair, in order

Before linking, the host tries each span (sop/repair.mjs repairSpan), in this order, and stops at the first that succeeds:

  1. dates and times, for a time hint or a span that reads as a date;
  2. numbers, money and units ("1.140 euro", "2380 lei", "12 km", number words up to twenty);
  3. a conversation reference ("that one", "him", "the earlier one"): resolved from the caller-owned context (the previous question's quoted values) only when exactly one candidate exists;
  4. a proper name: a surface the host lexicon knows as one entity, or a span of capitalized words;
  5. an English synonym: a common noun with exactly one synonym that the lexicon accepts.

A relation span is tried by the compiler against the near wire: repaired when that wire links, through the span's English synonyms or its own phrase. A repaired span fills its placeholder. An unresolved span becomes one clarification question in English ("Who or what do you mean by "…"?", "Which date or period do you mean by "…"?"; the output edge translates it); the wire it is paired with is held back, and every other wire of the turn is answered. The packet reports both:

  • repairs: [{unparsed, span, hint, near, wire, role, method, value, filled}], method one of time, number, quantity, conversation, lexicon_entity, proper_name, dictionary, near_wire_linked;
  • unresolved_spans: [{unparsed, span, hint, near, wire, role, blocking, question}]; blocking is true when a held-back wire depends on it, and then the packet adds next: "answer_clarification".

A number the model left as written

A span such as "twenty" with a number hint: the host reads it as 20 (repairs: [{"method": "number", "value": 20, "filled": true, …}]), fills the placeholder ?v and links the rest as usual.

A span the host cannot repair

EN “Maria works at the new place near the station. Who works at Alpha Lab?”

@s1 stated
  relation "work at"
  role subject "Maria"
  role object ?place
  polarity affirmed
  certainty asserted

@u1 unparsed
  span "the new place near the station"
  near $s1
  hint object

@q query
  select ?who
  where match
    relation "work at"
    role subject ?who
    role object "Alpha Lab"
    polarity affirmed
  end

No repair applies, so s1 is held back and the answer adds one question: "Who or what do you mean by "the new place near the station"?" (unresolved_spans, blocking: true). The question about Alpha Lab is still answered.

A time the question depends on

EN “Who worked at Alpha Lab on the day of the big outage?”

@q query
  select ?who
  where match
    relation "work at"
    role subject ?who
    role object "Alpha Lab"
    role time ?t
    polarity affirmed
  end

@u1 unparsed
  span "the day of the big outage"
  near $q
  hint time

The span pairs with the query's non-selected time variable ?t. It is not a date the host can read, so the only output is the question "Which date or period do you mean by "the day of the big outage"?". A readable date ("pe 1 martie 2026") would be repaired by method time.

A relation the model was unsure of

EN “Does Maria moonlight at Alpha Lab?”

@q query
  where match
    relation "work at"
    role subject "Maria"
    role object "Alpha Lab"
    polarity affirmed
  end

@u1 unparsed
  span "moonlight"
  near $q
  hint relation

The relation phrase is the model's best attempt; the span tells the host and the reader which word it did not map. The host also tries the span; here the query links, so the span is reported as repaired with method: "near_wire_linked".

Invalid model output

@u1 unparsed
  span moonlight

The span is one JSON-quoted string.

@u1 unparsed
  span "moonlight"
  hint verb

The hint comes from the closed set; the relation phrase is hint relation.

@u1 unparsed
  span "moonlight"
  reading "work at night"

The model does not interpret the span: a reading would be an assumed or an unclear kind ambiguous.

@u1 unparsed
  span "moonlight"
  near $s9
  hint relation

near names a wire of this output.

@s1 stated
  relation "work at"
  role subject "Maria"
  role object ?place
  polarity affirmed
  certainty asserted

@u1 unparsed
  span "the new place"
  near $s1
  hint object

@u2 unparsed
  span "near the station"
  near $u1

A span belongs to a formalized wire, not to another span; one span covers the whole unformalized phrase.

@s1 stated
  relation "work at"
  role subject "Maria"
  role object ?place
  polarity affirmed
  certainty asserted

@u1 unparsed
  span "the new place"
  near $s1
  hint object

@u2 unparsed
  span "The new place"

The same span is reported once.

@s1 stated
  relation "work at"
  role subject "Maria"
  role object ?place
  polarity affirmed
  certainty asserted

@u1 unparsed
  span "the new place"
  near $s1
  hint subject

The placeholder is the object; a span with hint subject does not pair with it, so the statement is not ground.
