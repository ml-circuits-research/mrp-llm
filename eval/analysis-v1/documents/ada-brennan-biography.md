# Ada Brennan (biographical note)

This note was written in 2025.

Ada Brennan was born in 1931 in a small fishing town. As of 2025 she is 96 years old.
Her elder brother Tom Brennan, born in 1927, is 141 years old according to the town register.

Ada Brennan never married.
She lived for forty years with her husband, the painter Leon Brennan.
A person who has a husband is married.

In 1960 she founded the Coastal Ecology Institute. The institute was founded on 1960-06-01 and dissolved on 1958-09-01.
