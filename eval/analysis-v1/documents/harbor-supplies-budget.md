# Harbor Street Office - Supplies Budget 2026

This budget refers to the year 2026.

The supplies budget for 2026 is 10000 EUR, up from 9000 EUR in 2025.
It is split into paper (4000 EUR), toner (3500 EUR) and other supplies (2500 EUR).
Paper accounts for 40% of the budget, toner for 35% and other supplies for 25%.

The paper line is larger than in 2025 because the office moves to printed contracts, as decided by the board in January 2026.
