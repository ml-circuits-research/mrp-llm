# HX-200 Circulation Pump - Operating Manual (excerpt)

## 1. Specifications

- Weight: 18 kg.
- Maximum operating pressure: 10 bar. Never operate the pump above this pressure.
- Hydraulic efficiency: 112% at the nominal point.
- Total electrical power draw: 1.5 kW, made up of the motor (1.2 kW) and the controller (0.3 kW).

## 2. Operation

The HX-200 is rated for continuous operation at 12 bar.
The pump must never run dry.
During commissioning the pump may run dry for up to 5 minutes.

## 3. Transport

The pump weighs 21 kg and must be lifted by two persons.
