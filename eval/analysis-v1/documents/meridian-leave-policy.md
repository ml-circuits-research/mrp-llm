# Meridian Logistics - Leave Policy 2026

This policy applies from 2026 and refers to the year 2026.

## Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave.
Part-time employees receive 50% of the full-time entitlement, that is 15 days.

## Who is covered

Contractors are not employees and are not covered by this policy.
Maria Lopez works as a contractor in the HR department.
Maria Lopez is an employee of Meridian Logistics.

## Requesting leave

A leave request must be submitted before it is approved.
