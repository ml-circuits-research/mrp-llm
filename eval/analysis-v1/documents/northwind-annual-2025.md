# Northwind Tools - Annual Report FY2025 (excerpt)

This report refers to the fiscal year 2025.

## Revenue

Total revenue in FY2025 was 12.4 million EUR, up from 11.0 million EUR in FY2024.
Revenue is split into three regions: Europe 6.1 million EUR, North America 4.2 million EUR and Asia-Pacific 1.6 million EUR.
By share of total revenue, Europe accounts for 49%, North America for 34% and Asia-Pacific for 13%.

## Profit and financing

Net profit was 0.93 million EUR.
Northwind Tools has no debt.
In March 2024 the company took a bank loan of 2.0 million EUR to finance the new plant.
A company that holds a bank loan has debt.

## Outlook

Because of the strong order book, management expects revenue to grow again in FY2026.
