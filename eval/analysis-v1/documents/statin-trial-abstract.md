# Low-dose Varostatin after myocardial infarction: a randomized trial (abstract)

Background. Patients recovering from a first myocardial infarction were eligible if they were aged 18 to 65 years.

Methods. We enrolled 240 patients: 130 were assigned to the treatment group and 120 to the control group.
Treatment-group patients made up 54% of the enrolled patients.
The trial ran from 2023-03-01 to 2022-11-30. The oldest enrolled patient was 71 years old.

Results. The response rate was 45% in the treatment group and 30% in the control group.

Conclusions. Since mortality was lower in the treatment group, this demonstrates that Varostatin reduces mortality after infarction.
