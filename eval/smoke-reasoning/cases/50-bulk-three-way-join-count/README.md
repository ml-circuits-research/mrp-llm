Bulk relational join. 3,000 orders, 150 customers and 12 regions generated by a fixed formula; a rule joins the three relations (`order_zone`) and the query counts the orders of one zone. A set-at-a-time engine runs the join as one statement; a nested-loop evaluator pays orders times customers. Expected count computed from the generator's formula. Feature: bulk joins over thousands of facts, exact count over closed relations.
