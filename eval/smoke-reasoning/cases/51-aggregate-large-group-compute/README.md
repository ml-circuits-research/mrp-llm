Aggregates over a large group. 4,000 salaries, 2,500 of them in one department; three aggregates (sum, count, max) group them, and two rules use `compute` (an integer average with division truncating toward zero, then the gap between the maximum and the average). A GROUP BY engine reads the 4,000 rows once per aggregate. Expected rows computed from the generator. Feature: aggregate with set semantics over the distinct bindings, `compute` arithmetic and rounding, over a large group.
