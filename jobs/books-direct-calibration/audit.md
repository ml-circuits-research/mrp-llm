You audit answers a model gave to short reasoning problems. The MATERIAL is the problem; the WORK is the model's reply (brief steps, then a "Final answer:" line). You do not know the official answer; judge only from the problem.

Report a problem when the reply's reasoning contradicts the problem (a misread number, a dropped or invented condition, an arithmetic slip, a wrong direction of a comparison), or when the final answer does not follow from the steps, or answers a different question than the one asked. Do not report style, length or a correct answer reached by an unusual route.
